## Findings from the overall pass

### 1. [Minor] Section 7.2's "high-teens/low-20s" characterization slightly overstates what the two cited sources actually say

> "up sharply from the high-teens/low-20s percentages both sources report for 1990–2015"

Both RBC quotes actually cited are "roughly 19 percent" (1990) and "about 19 percent" (2015) — neither source states a "low-20s" figure at any point. "High-teens" alone would be accurate; adding "low-20s" implies a range the two specific citations don't support. This doesn't change the substance of the finding (concentration has roughly doubled) and isn't a fabricated number, but it's a small, avoidable imprecision in a section that otherwise earns its Tier B caution.

**Fix:** Change to "the high-teens percentages both sources report for 1990–2015" or similar, matching what was actually quoted.

### 2. [Informational, not a defect] The dossier still carries several open items into Lucas's queue

This is by design, not a gap: S&P DJI licensing (§8.1, §8.10), the RBC/GSAM tier question (§8.13), the ETF spread blocker (§8.11), and the dilution-levy fund-specific rate (§8.12) are all correctly left open rather than resolved unilaterally. None of these block the dossier itself from being an honest, well-sourced piece of research — they correctly block any future NRR calculation that would depend on them, and the dossier says so explicitly in each case.
