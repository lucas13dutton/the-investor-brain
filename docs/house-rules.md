## Part 1: House rules

### What we are

A research and education company. We report what happened to different assets historically, what it cost to own them, and how we worked it out.

### What we never do

1. **No personal recommendations.** Never say or imply that a viewer, or a type of viewer, should buy, sell or hold anything.
2. **No calls on specific investments.** No "buy", "sell", "undervalued", "overvalued", "about to explode" or price targets for any specific share, fund, coin, property or collectible.
3. **No signals.** No alerts, copy-trading, "what I'm buying this week" or timed entries and exits.
4. **No real-money trading on behalf of the company or audience.** The Strategy Lab uses paper portfolios only, labelled every time.
5. **No promises.** Never suggest a return is likely, safe or guaranteed. Never say "passive income", "easy money" or "can't lose".
6. **No flexing.** No lifestyle shots, cars, cash piles or income screenshots to imply results.
7. **No paid promotion of investments.** No affiliate links, sponsorships or referral codes for investment products, platforms, dealers or unregulated schemes until a compliance review approves a specific arrangement.
8. **No personal questions answered personally.** Comments like "should I sell my gold?" get the standard reply (Part 3), never an answer.
9. **No naming and shaming individuals** without a published official source (a court, regulator or government release).
10. **No made-up numbers.** Every figure comes from the evidence log.

### What we always do

1. State the **window** and **cost scenario** for every return shown.
2. Show **costs and losers**, not just winners.
3. Include the **risk line** inside the video itself, visible on screen or spoken.
4. **Label** paper portfolios, event windows and best-case samples clearly.
5. **Correct publicly** when we get something wrong.
6. Be **transparent about the AI**: say what agents did and what Lucas decided.

### Mandatory risk lines

Each line below must appear **verbatim** — word for word, not paraphrased — in the format and position stated. A close rewording does not satisfy this.

**1. Short-form video** — on screen for at least 2 seconds, not caption only:
> Education, not advice. Past returns don't predict future ones. Investments can fall as well as rise.

**2. Long-form video** — spoken once near the start:
> This is research and education, not financial advice. What happened in the past doesn't tell you what happens next, and you can get back less than you put in.

**3. Newsletter and website footer** — footer of every newsletter issue and every website page:
> Assayer is a research and education publisher. Nothing here is financial advice or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any investment. Past performance is not a guide to future performance. The value of investments can fall as well as rise, and you may get back less than you invested.

"Assayer" is a provisional brand name pending the final name — update this line when the name is finalised.

**4. App, on any comparison result** — attached to every comparison result shown:
> Historical comparison only, after the costs shown. Different costs, dates or tax circumstances give different results.

**5. Any Strategy Lab output** — attached every time a Strategy Lab result appears:
> Paper portfolio for research. No real money. Not a signal to act.

## Part 2: Compliance checklist

The Compliance agent runs this on every script, caption, thumbnail, newsletter and web page before Lucas sees it. Every line must be a **Pass**, or the piece goes back to the Writer with the failed line numbers.

### A. Advice and promotion

- [ ] A1. No wording tells or nudges anyone to buy, sell or hold a specific investment.
- [ ] A2. No wording tailored to a viewer's personal circumstances ("if you're 30 with £10k, you should...").
- [ ] A3. No price targets, "undervalued"/"overvalued" or predictions about a specific investment.
- [ ] A4. No promised, likely or "typical" returns presented as what the viewer will get.
- [ ] A5. No get-rich-quick, "passive income" or "can't lose" language, including in the title, thumbnail and hook.
- [ ] A6. No affiliate links, referral codes or sponsor mentions for investment products.

### B. Numbers and evidence

- [ ] B1. Every number has an evidence log ID.
- [ ] B2. Every return states its window and cost scenario.
- [ ] B3. Event windows are labelled and paired with a standard window.
- [ ] B4. Item-based assets (LEGO, cards, watches) show the median and share of losers, not just the average.
- [ ] B5. No Tier C source is the only support for any number.
- [ ] B6. Charts start their axes honestly and show the same window as the claim.

### C. Labels and warnings

- [ ] C1. The correct mandatory risk line for this format (see "Mandatory risk lines" above) appears verbatim, in the position required — not only in the caption or description.
- [ ] C2. Paper portfolios carry the paper portfolio label every time they appear.
- [ ] C3. AI involvement is described accurately (no overstating what the agents did).
- [ ] C4. The risk line is legible on a phone screen and sits in a fixed position that doesn't compete with the figures on screen.

### D. Fairness and people

- [ ] D1. Claims about named companies or people are backed by an official or Tier A/B source.
- [ ] D2. Scam and fraud content cites official findings (court, regulator, Insolvency Service) and uses "alleged" where findings aren't final.
- [ ] D3. No copied scripts, footage or thumbnails from other creators. Signal Room patterns are adapted, never reproduced.

### E. Tone

- [ ] E1. Calm referee, not hype. No fear or envy used to push action.
- [ ] E2. The title and thumbnail promise only what the content delivers.

### Result

- **Pass:** all lines pass, send to Lucas for final approval.
- **Fix:** list the failed line numbers and a suggested rewrite for each.
- **Escalate:** anything the checklist doesn't cover, or any doubt, goes to Lucas with a note. When unsure, the answer is no until Lucas decides.

## Part 3: Replying to comments and messages

**Someone asks what they should do with their money:**
> We can't give personal advice. That needs someone who knows your situation, and in the UK, a regulated adviser. The full costs and data are in the video and on our site if you want to dig in.

**Someone asks what we're buying:**
> We don't share personal trades or tips. The Strategy Lab runs paper portfolios for research only.

**Someone disputes a number:**
> Thanks. Every number links to its source in our evidence log. If we've got it wrong, we'll correct it publicly.

## Part 4: Escalation list

Always goes to Lucas before anything is published or sent:

- Any mention of a specific listed company, fund or coin.
- Any scam, fraud or legal story.
- Any partnership, sponsorship or paid offer.
- Any complaint, correction request or message from a regulator, lawyer or journalist.
- Anything the Compliance agent marks "Escalate".
