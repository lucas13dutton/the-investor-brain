# Media plan

**Status: draft plan.** Phase 0 runs to 31 October 2026, with nothing published. Everything below is the target shape once regular publishing begins, not a record of what has already gone out.

---

## Phase 0 (now – 31 October 2026)

No content publishes during Phase 0. This period is for building the brain (dossiers, cost models, the evidence log), pre-registering the first Strategy Lab tests, and producing a backlog, so that when publishing starts it starts from a position of strength rather than catching up live.

---

## The five series

| Series | Format | Cadence (once live) | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Research** | Long-form film, YouTube | Weekly | The flagship: one asset or question investigated in depth, drawing directly on a dossier and its evidence log. The source material every other series' output that week is repurposed from. |
| **Asset Autopsy** | Short-form vertical video | Several per week (see repurposing rule) | "Compare assets" — head-to-head comparisons, mostly cut down from that week's Research film, plus the occasional standalone short pairing. Shares its name with the comparison feature (`docs/products.md`) deliberately. |
| **Strategy Lab** | Long-form film, as results land | Irregular — tied to when a pre-registered test reaches a verdict, not a fixed weekly slot | Reports one paper-portfolio strategy's kept-or-killed result, per `docs/methodology.md` section 9. Cadence is set by how long a strategy takes to reach an out-of-sample verdict, not by the calendar. |
| **Build Log** | Short-form video / written post | One per Research film | Build-in-public: what the team (agents and Lucas) actually did that week — a look behind the research and production process itself, generated as a repurposing output of that week's Research film. |
| **Scam Watch** | Short or mid-form video | As warranted, not scheduled | Covers a specific scam, fraud pattern or misleading promotion. Every episode is a house-rules Part 4 escalation item — it goes to Lucas before publishing regardless of the regular schedule, and only proceeds on an official source per house-rules D2. |

## Platform roles

| Platform | Role |
|---|---|
| **YouTube** | Home for long-form: The Research and Strategy Lab. Also carries the short-form output (Shorts) for Asset Autopsy and Build Log. |
| **TikTok** | Short-form only: Asset Autopsy comparison clips, Build Log clips. |
| **Instagram** | Short-form (Reels) mirroring TikTok, plus the LinkedIn-style carousel adapted as a static/carousel post where it fits the format. |
| **LinkedIn** | The weekly carousel repurposed from that week's Research film — the more analytical, professional-toned surface. |
| **X** | The weekly thread repurposed from that week's Research film, plus any time-sensitive Scam Watch commentary. |
| **Newsletter** | One issue per Research film, plus the footer risk line on every issue per `docs/house-rules.md`. |

---

## Repurposing rule

Each long-form film (from The Research) yields:

- 4–6 shorts (Asset Autopsy)
- 1 LinkedIn carousel
- 1 X thread
- 1 newsletter issue
- 1 Build Log

Nothing here is a separate research effort — it's the same underlying dossier and evidence log, cut for each platform's format. Every piece still goes through `docs/house-rules.md` compliance (each format carries its own mandatory risk line, per house-rules "Mandatory risk lines") before it publishes.

## Weekly publishing schedule (once live)

| Day | Publishes |
|---|---|
| Monday | The Research (long-form film), YouTube |
| Monday | Newsletter issue for that film |
| Tuesday–Friday | Asset Autopsy shorts (4–6, spread across the week), YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels |
| Wednesday | LinkedIn carousel |
| Thursday | X thread |
| Friday | Build Log |
| No fixed day | Strategy Lab (on verdict) and Scam Watch (as warranted) — both publish out of cycle when ready, not on this weekly grid |

This is the target rhythm once publishing starts; it will be adjusted once the actual production pace is known.
